Gadget Dashboard

This project involved designing and developing a fully interactive Gadgets Sales Dashboard using Power BI to analyse sales performance across multiple regions, customer demographics, and product brands. Working with a dataset spanning revenue, cost of goods sold, and customer engagement metrics, I performed end-to-end data cleaning, transformation, and visualisation to build a dynamic reporting solution. The dashboard features region and gender filters, KPI cards, bar charts, donut charts, and an area chart enabling users to slice and explore data from multiple angles and derive meaningful business conclusions.

The analysis uncovered several critical business insights, including Apple's dominance as the highest-performing brand with $197K in profit, and the identification of medium income customers as the primary profit driver, contributing 37.23% of total profits. The dashboard also revealed a seasonal dip in customer engagement during February, dropping to 1,719 before recovering to 1,740 in March, pointing to an opportunity for targeted marketing campaigns during low engagement periods. With total revenue of $3.11M against $2.17M in COGS, the dashboard further highlighted the need for margin optimisation strategies. This project demonstrates my ability to transform raw data into actionable business intelligence that supports strategic decision making.

Sales Dashboard

This project demonstrates my ability to handle large datasets, build professional dashboards, and extract meaningful business insights that drive strategic decisions. Betting Dashboard

This project presents an interactive data analysis dashboard examining betting addiction patterns among Nigerians across different age groups, regions, and betting platforms. Using a sample size of 2,000 respondents, the analysis explores average weekly betting behaviour, addiction scores, and platform preferences to uncover the social and demographic patterns driving gambling habits across Nigeria. The dashboard was built entirely in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, charts, and slicers to deliver a clean, interactive, and insightful reporting solution.

coffee Dashboard

Built using Power BI, this Coffee Order Analysis Dashboard transforms over 1 million coffee orders into a visually compelling and fully interactive business intelligence solution. The project covers sales performance across three international markets the United States, Ireland, and the United Kingdom spanning a four year period from 2019 to 2022. By integrating dynamic year sliders, loyalty card filters, and multi dimensional charts, the dashboard empowers business stakeholders to explore revenue trends, customer preferences, and product performance at the click of a button. From data collection and cleaning through to transformation and visualisation, every stage of the analytical process was executed entirely within Excel demonstrating the full power of the tool in the hands of a skilled analyst.

The insights drawn from this analysis paint a vivid picture of the coffee retail landscape. The United States stands as the dominant market with an overwhelming 774K in orders, dwarfing Ireland and the United Kingdom combined yet this gap also signals untapped growth potential in international markets. Light roast coffee proved to be the customer favourite, capturing 38.45% of total revenue, while Excelsa edged out competitors as the highest earning coffee type at $12.3K. Perhaps the most actionable finding is the sharp revenue decline in August, plummeting to just $2.3K against a peak of $4.8K in both March and June a seasonal pattern that presents a clear opportunity for targeted marketing and promotional interventions to stabilise year-round revenue performance.
